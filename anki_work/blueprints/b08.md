## 08 M2 study guide checklist (cram ≤12 high)
1. [high] Define speedup $S$ and efficiency $E$; state linear speedup. (evidence: rung 1 p.1–2)
   Full-mark: $S=T_{\text{serial}}/T_{\text{parallel}}$; $E=S/p$; linear $S=p$ ($E=1$).
2. [high] Write Amdahl’s law and limit $1/a$. (evidence: rung 1 p.2)
   Full-mark: $S=1/((1-a)/p+a)$; as $p\to\infty$, $S\to 1/a$.
3. [high] Why $E$ falls as $p$ grows. (evidence: rung 1 p.2)
   Full-mark: overheads grow with $p$ (thread mgmt, mutexes, network/file I/O).
4. [high] Memory/communication: Pthreads vs MPI vs Warp. (evidence: rung 1 p.3)
   Full-mark: shared addr+sync; separate processes+messages; host/device copies, kernels on device mem.
5. [high] Execution targets for the three models. (evidence: rung 1 p.3)
   Full-mark: shared-mem threads; multi-node CPU processes; CPU host+NVIDIA GPU (or CPU fallback).
6. [high] Built-ins: barriers/DIY vs collectives vs kernel launches. (evidence: rung 1 p.4)
   Full-mark: Pthreads barriers+DIY reductions; MPI rich collectives; Warp launches over $N$.
7. [high] Best-fit workloads for each. (evidence: rung 1 p.4)
   Full-mark: fine-grained shared updates; large chunks+modest comm; large uniform data-parallel maps that pay for copies.
8. [high] Signature pitfalls for each. (evidence: rung 1 p.4)
   Full-mark: races/deadlocks/oversubscription; mismatched tags/deadlocks/small messages; excess copies/non-contiguous/too-small work.
