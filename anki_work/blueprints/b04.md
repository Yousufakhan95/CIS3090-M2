## 04 MPI performance (Ch3 §3.6) — cram

Hard limit: 8 high-yield cards only.

1. [high] $S=T_{\mathrm{serial}}/T_{\mathrm{parallel}}$, $E=S/p$; linear speedup $S=p$. (study guide; §3.6.3)
2. [high] Use wall-clock (MPI Wtime), not CPU time — idle/wait counts. (§3.6.1)
3. [high] One parallel time: MPI Barrier → Wtime → Reduce with MPI MAX. (§3.6.1)
4. [high] Report minimum run-time across runs. (§3.6.1)
5. [high] $T_{\mathrm{parallel}}=T_{\mathrm{serial}}/p+T_{\mathrm{overhead}}$; MPI overhead ≈ communication. (§3.6.2)
6. [high] Small $n$, large $p$: overhead dominates (little speedup). (§3.6.2–3)
7. [high] Practice compute $S$ and $E$ from times (study-guide Q5).
8. [high] Scalable: grow problem size with $p$ so efficiency does not fall. (§3.6.4)
