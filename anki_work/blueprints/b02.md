## 02 Performance / Amdahl (Ch2 §2.6) — cram

Hard cap: ≤10 high-yield only. Exam signals: \(S=T_{\text{serial}}/T_{\text{parallel}}\), \(E=S/p\), linear \(S=p\), overheads grow with \(p\), Amdahl \(S=1/((1-a)/p+a)\) with limit \(1/a\); practice Q5.

1. [high] Define speedup \(S\). Full-mark: \(S=T_{\text{serial}}/T_{\text{parallel}}\).
2. [high] What is linear speedup? Full-mark: \(S=p\) (ideal equal split, no overhead).
3. [high] Define efficiency \(E\). Full-mark: \(E=S/p=T_{\text{serial}}/(p\cdot T_{\text{parallel}})\).
4. [high] Why does \(E\) usually fall as \(p\) grows? Full-mark: parallel overhead (mutex/critical sections, network, thread mgmt, I/O) grows with \(p\), so \(S\) is a smaller fraction of ideal \(p\).
5. [high] State Amdahl’s law with serial fraction \(a\). Full-mark: \(S=1/((1-a)/p+a)\).
6. [high] Amdahl limit as \(p\to\infty\). Full-mark: \(S\to 1/a\).
7. [high] \(a=0.1\) (90% parallelized): best \(S\)? Full-mark: \(S\le 10\).
8. [high] Practice: 100 s serial; 40 s on 4 cores → \(S\) and \(E\)? Full-mark: \(S=2.5\), \(E=0.625\).
9. [high] Practice: 100 s serial; 25 s on 8 cores → \(S\) and \(E\)? Full-mark: \(S=4\), \(E=0.5\) (speedup up, efficiency down vs 4 cores).
10. [high] Decomposition with overhead. Full-mark: \(T_{\text{parallel}}=T_{\text{serial}}/p+T_{\text{overhead}}\).
